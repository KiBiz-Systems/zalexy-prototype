Admin

Intake & Form Builder

Build the client intake questionnaire from reusable question groups and subgroups. A question connects to a group, and the client's answer opens the matching subgroup — no conditions to write. Several named forms are assembled from the same shared library, with a client-facing live preview.

Admin

Policy Engine Settings

Configure hierarchical business rules for locations, services and funding on a 4-level inheritance model. Owns the scheduling policy set: cancellation windows and credit rules per funding type, the make-up token policy, voucher make-up modes, the consecutive late-cancel rule, waitlist mode and funding-override duration modes. Every setting states in one plain sentence what changes, and on which screen, when it is edited.

Billing: Failed Payments

The queue where money that did not arrive waits for a person. Three tabs with three distinct column sets — retries exhausted, retries pending, and closed cases. Staff

Funding: Regional Center Authorizations

The Billing Admin's control screen for Regional Center funding. Every KPI tile filters the table it counts; an authorization under override says so on the row and opens with a banner naming who overrode it, why, and until when. A pending authorization with a preferred recurring slot reads Tentative, and an expired one reads Needs Revalidation — both computed from the recurring schedule, with nothing reserved and no per-occurrence record. The proration panel shows the arithmetic that produced the first-period units.

Scheduling · Staff

Calendar Master Schedule

The staff pivot grid — coaches as columns, time slots as rows, both axes sticky, with five working filters and distinct data per day. Every booking state has its own block treatment and says what it is in words, not colour alone. The detail drawer carries a cancellation policy preview that states early-or-late and the exact credit consequence before you confirm, make-up token generation, real check-in and no-show marking, the manager-approved waitlist loop, a consecutive late-cancel warning and a manager funding override. Recurring slots pending authorization are computed from the schedule and shown as Tentative — nothing is reserved.

Scheduling · Staff

Staff / Manager Booking Flow

A five-step booking wizard for Group, Semi-Private and Private sessions — Who, When & with whom, Preview, Funding, Summary. Up to three customers as tabs, each with up to three services; dry/wet service order is enforced and overlapping picks are warned and refused. Classes are grouped day → instructor → slot, with seat-by-seat capacity and the allowed level, age group and location on every card. Preview lists the real generated dates as a list or a calendar, stopping at the active payment term and marking anything past it Tentative. Funding is its own step per service — Regional Center auth code, voucher, make-up or card/cash/check — and the summary prints.

Scheduling · Client

Client Portal Booking Flow

A five-step wizard built so a parent booking a first lesson needs no help — Who is this for, When would you like to come, Preview, How you'll pay, Your booking. Up to three household members as tabs, and no coach names anywhere. Says what a booking reserves, what attending uses and what cancelling in time returns, all in plain words. Preview shows the real dates as a list or a calendar and says plainly where the current plan stops. Buy sessions inline when the balance runs out, spend a make-up token, and print the finished booking to keep.
